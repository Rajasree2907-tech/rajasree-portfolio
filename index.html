Academic Research Project

Explainable AI-Based PCOS Prediction Using Machine Learning and Large Language Models

Research Setting

Carried out during my full-time internship at NIT Puducherry under Dr. Ansuman Mahapatra, Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

Objectives & Approach

  • Predictive modeling: Evaluated gradient-boosted tree models (CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost) on tabular clinical data.
  • Explainable AI: Used SHAP for global feature ranking and patient-level prediction explanations.
  • LLM decision support: Turned feature contributions into structured, readable summaries for clinicians.
Notice: An academic demonstration and decision-support prototype. It has not been clinically validated and is not intended to replace medical professionals.
AI for Sustainability

ARCOVA — AI-Powered Evacuation Risk and Routing System

Concept

ARCOVA helps emergency teams predict hazardous road conditions, plan dynamic evacuation routes, and spread shelter demand across relief hubs.

Core Capabilities

  • Disaster risk modeling: ML on environmental factors to flag vulnerable road segments.
  • Dynamic routing: Balances route safety against travel time.
  • Shelter balancing: Assigns evacuees to shelters to prevent overcrowding.
  • Web interface: Python/Flask prototype with map visualization.
Notice: A conceptual prototype and simulation. It does not connect to live emergency dispatch systems.
